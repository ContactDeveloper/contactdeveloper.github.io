1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect your name, address, phone number or email inside the Apps. However, the Apps use third-party services that may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, time zone)

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, which we use only to reply to you.

4. App Tracking Transparency and your choices

On iOS 14.5 and later, the Apps ask your permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also limit ad personalization in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), we ask for your consent before personalized advertising, and you may withdraw it at any time.

5. Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and security

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email us. We do not sell your personal information for money.
